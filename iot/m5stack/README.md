# M5Stack ESP32 Matter devices

[Flipped Energy M5Stack firmware](https://github.com/flipped-energy/m5stack) displays your tariff, prices and usage and exposes energy signals through Matter.

Officially supported boards:

- [M5Stack Core2 for AWS](coreaws/README.md)
- [M5Stack Dial](dial/README.md)

Use the firmware and setup instructions for your exact board from the [firmware repository](https://github.com/flipped-energy/m5stack). Create a [read-only token](https://flipped.energy/accounts/developer?tab=tokens) for the device. The token gives it access to your own account data.
